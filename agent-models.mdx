IQX lets you choose which model does the work. That choice is the single biggest
lever on what you spend, because **credits are charged per iteration, and the rate
is set by the mode you picked**.

## The modes

| Mode | Credits per iteration | Available on | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **DeepSeek V4 Flash** | 0.5 | Free | Fast agentic edits, quick iteration |
| **IQX Spark** | 1 | Free | Focused edits and lightweight builds |
| **DeepSeek V4 Pro** | 2.5 | Pro | Larger coding tasks at low cost |
| **Kimi K3** | 3 | Pro | Frontend and design specialist |
| **IQX Nova** | 3 | Pro | The balanced default for polished product work |
| **IQX Ultimate** | 5 | Pro | Deep autonomy for ambitious execution |
| **Claude Opus 5** | 5 | Pro | The hardest agentic work |

<Note>
Free accounts build with **DeepSeek V4 Flash** and **IQX Spark**. Everything else
unlocks on Pro and above. See [Plans and credits](/plans-and-credits).
</Note>

## What an iteration is

An iteration is one turn of agent work: it reads, decides, and acts. A single
prompt from you almost never takes one iteration. **A typical prompt runs 2 to 5
iterations**, depending on how much the agent has to read and how many files it
has to change.

So the cost of a prompt is:

```
credits = mode rate x iterations
```

A prompt on IQX Nova (3 credits) that takes 4 iterations costs 12 credits. The
same prompt on DeepSeek V4 Flash (0.5) costs 2.

<Tip>
Reading files, opening the preview, browsing your project, and running the app do
not cost credits. Only agent iterations do.
</Tip>

## What 500 credits actually buys

Pro includes 500 credits per month. At 2 to 5 iterations per prompt, that is
roughly:

| Mode | Credits per prompt | Prompts per 500 credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DeepSeek V4 Flash | 1 to 2.5 | ~200 to 500 |
| IQX Spark | 2 to 5 | ~100 to 250 |
| DeepSeek V4 Pro | 5 to 12.5 | ~40 to 100 |
| Kimi K3 / IQX Nova | 6 to 15 | ~33 to 83 |
| IQX Ultimate / Claude Opus 5 | 10 to 25 | ~20 to 50 |

Most people do not spend a month on one mode. A realistic month is the bulk of
your work on Nova, small edits dropped to Flash or Spark, and Ultimate saved for
the few problems that genuinely need it. Mixed like that, 500 credits covers a
lot of building.

## Reasoning effort

Separately from the mode, you set how hard the agent thinks before it acts:

| Effort | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Low** | Fastest, lowest token spend. Best for small scoped tasks |
| **Medium** | Balanced speed, cost, and reasoning depth |
| **High** | Strong reasoning, the provider default for coding work |

Effort does not change the per-iteration rate, but higher effort means more
thinking tokens per iteration and often fewer wasted iterations on hard problems.
On a trivial edit it is pure overhead; on an ambiguous one it can save you a
failed attempt.

## Picking well

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Rename a button, fix copy, adjust spacing">
    **DeepSeek V4 Flash or IQX Spark, low effort.** Around 1 to 5 credits. This is
    mechanical work, and a frontier model adds cost without adding quality.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add a feature to an existing app">
    **IQX Nova, medium effort.** Around 6 to 15 credits. The agent has to read your
    code and fit into it, which needs real comprehension but not deep planning.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Build a frontend or polish a design">
    **Kimi K3, medium effort.** Same 3 credit rate as Nova, tuned for interface and
    design work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Design the data model, or fix a bug you cannot reproduce">
    **IQX Ultimate or Claude Opus 5, high effort.** Around 10 to 25 credits. These
    are the cases where the extra credits buy a genuinely better answer, and where
    a cheap mode often costs more by failing twice first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Where to set it

The mode and effort picker sits in the chat input in the Studio. Your choice is
saved to your account and carries across projects and sessions until you change
it, so set it once and adjust per task.

<Warning>
If your balance runs low, modes you can no longer afford are locked in the picker
rather than failing mid-run. Top up with an [add-on pack](/plans-and-credits) to
unlock them again.
</Warning>
