IQX plans provide an AI credit allowance and access to plan features. Agent credits, production hosting and external provider charges are separate parts of the cost of building and running an app.

## Monthly plans

The catalog below was reviewed on September 11, 2026. Amounts are in EUR. Use [live pricing](https://iqx.dev/pricing) and checkout for the offer, tax and billing interval that apply to your account.

The consumer Pro price is currently displayed as **€25 including 25% Swedish VAT**. Business prices are displayed excluding VAT. Your checkout determines applicable tax; do not compare an inclusive consumer price with an exclusive business price as though they use the same tax basis.

Annual subscriptions use the annual price shown at checkout and are billed annually. Review both the total charge and the credit renewal schedule before choosing an interval.

## Model and collaboration access

Free supports the basic model set listed in [Agent models](/agent-models). Pro and higher plans unlock the paid model catalog, subject to current availability.

Pro supports 5 collaborators and 5 viewers. Business supports 15 collaborators and 5 viewers. Shared-credit controls apply to Business and Enterprise where enabled, rather than every paid collaboration project. See [Collaboration](/collaboration).

Enterprise requirements are discussed with IQX. Do not assume that an enterprise inquiry automatically enables a particular compliance certification, SSO setup or custom deployment arrangement.

## Add-on credits

The current quantity-based add-on is **1,000 credits for €15 excluding VAT**. Checkout supports 1–10 packs per purchase. These purchased lots have a 12-month validity period, and subscription credits are consumed before eligible add-on credits.

Monthly plan credits reset with their credit period and do not accumulate indefinitely. Add-ons are tracked separately and remain usable until spent or expired. Check the balance, renewal and expiry information for your account, especially if it has older purchases or a custom arrangement.

## What credits cover

Credits pay for billable agent work at the selected model's effective rate. Media tools can add credit charges. The number of prompts is not a fixed entitlement because different tasks require different amounts of work.

Production services and third-party subscriptions are separate. Continue with [Usage and billing](/usage-and-billing) before estimating the cost of a deployed app.
