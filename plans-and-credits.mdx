## How credits are consumed

Credits are charged per **agent iteration**, not per message, and the rate is set
by the mode you build with:

| Mode | Credits per iteration |
| --- | --- |
| DeepSeek V4 Flash | 0.5 |
| IQX Spark | 1 |
| DeepSeek V4 Pro | 2.5 |
| Kimi K3 | 3 |
| IQX Nova | 3 |
| IQX Ultimate | 5 |
| Claude Opus 5 | 5 |

One prompt from you usually takes the agent **2 to 5 iterations**, so:

```
credits = mode rate x iterations
```

A prompt on IQX Nova that runs 4 iterations costs 12 credits. The same prompt on
DeepSeek V4 Flash costs 2.

### What 500 credits buys

| Mode | Prompts per 500 credits |
| --- | --- |
| DeepSeek V4 Flash | ~200 to 500 |
| IQX Spark | ~100 to 250 |
| DeepSeek V4 Pro | ~40 to 100 |
| Kimi K3 / IQX Nova | ~33 to 83 |
| IQX Ultimate / Claude Opus 5 | ~20 to 50 |

In practice you mix them: most work on Nova, small edits on Flash or Spark, and
Ultimate saved for the hard problems. Mixed like that, 500 credits covers a lot.

<Tip>
Cost follows the mode, so pick deliberately. Full guidance in
[Agent modes and credit cost](/agent-models).
</Tip>

Reading files, browsing your project, opening the preview, and running the app do
not cost credits. Only agent iterations do.

### Tools that cost extra

A small number of tools call expensive external services, and those bill on top of
the iteration cost. Today that is **3D asset generation at 20 credits per asset
produced**. If a batch partly fails, you are only charged for the assets that were
actually generated.

Everything else the agent does, including editing files, running commands, and
deploying services, is covered by the iteration cost alone.

## Add-on credits

Run out mid-month? Pro and Business accounts can top up at any time.

Add-on credits are sold in **packs of 250 for €15** excluding VAT, and you can buy
**1 to 10 packs in one checkout**, so up to 2,500 credits for €150.

That works out to a flat **€0.06 per credit**, with no volume discount at any
quantity. That is deliberate: Pro works out to €0.04 per credit, so the add-on
rate sits above the subscription rate on purpose. Buying a plan is always the
cheaper way to get credits, and a top-up never undercuts it.

Three things worth knowing:

- **Plan credits are spent first.** Your add-on balance is only drawn down once
  the monthly allowance is gone, so topping up early never wastes anything.
- **Add-on credits roll over for 12 months.** Unlike plan credits, which reset
  each cycle, a top-up stays on your account for a year from purchase.
- **Add-ons need a paid plan.** Free accounts cannot spend add-on credits, so the
  purchase option only appears on Pro and above.

## When credits reset

**Plan credits reset monthly** and do not roll over. Whatever you have not used in
a cycle is gone at the start of the next one.

**Add-on credits do not reset.** They sit on top of your plan allowance and stay
until spent, up to the 12 month window above.

## Paying yearly

Pro and Business can be billed annually at **10% off** the monthly rate. Pro is
€216 per year instead of €240.

## Changing plan

Upgrading takes effect immediately, and this is the good part: whatever plan
credits you had not used are converted into add-on credits and carried over, and
your new plan starts with a full fresh allowance rather than a prorated one. You
lose nothing by upgrading mid-cycle.

Downgrading is scheduled rather than immediate. You keep the higher plan until the
end of the current billing cycle, then move down.

## Deployment costs

Deployments are billed separately from credits, pay as you go, starting at
**€0.0569 per hour** for a 1 CPU / 2 GB RAM / 30 GB instance. You are billed for
what runs, so stopping a deployment stops its cost. See
[Deployments](/deployments).
