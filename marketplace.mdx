IQX's community and marketplace help you discover projects and share your own work. A public showcase, a paid template and your private working project serve different purposes.

## Explore a project

Open the community from the [dashboard](https://iqx.dev/dashboard?view=community) or visit the [marketplace](https://iqx.dev/launchpad). Read the description and inspect the available preview before choosing a project.

Check what the project actually includes: its screens, framework, required services, provider integrations and setup instructions. A working public demo does not mean that its private credentials or production data transfer to your copy.

## Start from a template

Use the available purchase or project-creation flow for the selected listing. Open the resulting project in Studio, inspect the source and configure your own [services](/services), [integrations](/integrations) and [environment variables](/environment-variables).

Replace demo content and test the main workflow before [publishing](/deployments). Check the listing's terms and included assets rather than assuming every template has identical rights or support.

## Prepare a project to sell

The current creator offering requires **Pro or above** to unlock selling. Prepare a useful project with a working preview, an accurate description and clear setup requirements. Remove private data and secrets from any source or assets that will be shared.

Set the available listing details and EUR price, then submit through the project's publishing flow. Paid marketplace submissions are reviewed before being made available. Submission is not immediate approval.

## Creator payments

The public [creator program](https://iqx.dev/partners) advertises an **80% creator share**. Payout setup uses Stripe Connect and requires the requested identity and bank information. Eligible payouts follow the program's monthly process; verify the current terms and your account status for the amount and timing that apply to you.

A listed price does not guarantee sales or a payout. External verification requirements and the applicable marketplace terms still matter.

## Keep a listing useful

When you change a template, keep the description, preview and setup instructions aligned with what a buyer receives. Document external provider requirements and any work needed to launch. For general project sharing with teammates, use [Collaboration](/collaboration).
